Important Usage Notes

        
    
          
  • Always keep the generated PNG file as your original encoded image.
    • 
          
  • JPEG compression, resizing, screenshots, filters, and image editing can destroy hidden data.
    • 
          
  • Do not convert the generated PNG into another image format.
    • 
          
  • Use a strong, unique password and keep it safe. A lost password cannot be recovered.
    • 
          
  • Encryption protects message confidentiality and detects ciphertext tampering, but does not hide the fact that an image contains a payload.
